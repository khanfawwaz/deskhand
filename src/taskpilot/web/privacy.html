Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026. Applies to the Deskhand web app, the Deskhand app for Windows, the Deskhand server at deskhand.fawwazkhan.dev and the Deskhand Chrome extension.

The short version

  • Deskhand is a small independent project, free of charge. Nobody pays for it, and your data is not the product.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or run analytics or tracking. There are no cookies.
  • To do a task, Deskhand sends the text it needs (and sometimes a picture of the window it is working in) to an AI model provider. Details below.
  • You can download or delete everything yourself in Settings, under "Your data".
  • Finished tasks are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Who runs this

Deskhand is a small, independent, non-commercial project (github.com/khanfawwaz/deskhand). The person who runs the Deskhand server is the one responsible for the data on it. For deskhand.fawwazkhan.dev that is the project's owner, running it on their own computer. If you run your own copy of the Deskhand server, you are the one responsible for it, and this policy is only a starting point for yours.

Private by default. Deskhand only works on the apps, folders and websites you have approved, and asks you before a new website. It is built so that windows and pages outside those are not read or sent anywhere.

Your computer and the Chrome extension

  • The Deskhand app for Windows keeps its settings, a sign-in token for your computer and its own log on your computer. It acts only inside the approved apps, folders and websites in its safety rules.
  • The Chrome extension collects nothing itself and sends nothing to us. It talks only to the Deskhand app on the same computer (never over the internet), makes no requests to the internet and loads no remote code. While a task you started is running, it passes that tab's address, title, visible text and links to the app on your computer and carries out the clicks and typing the app asks for. It does nothing until you press "Connect to Deskhand" and approve it in a window on your computer, and you can disconnect at any time. Chrome shows its standard "is debugging this browser" bar while it works.
  • What the extension is allowed to do: debugger (read the task's tab and click or type in it), tabs (find that tab and read its address and title), alarms (stay connected to the app on your computer) and storage (remember that you approved the connection). It does not ask for cookie access.

Cookies and browser storage

The Deskhand web app sets no cookies. It keeps three small items in your browser's local storage: pilot.token (keeps you signed in on this device), pilot.targets (which computers you picked) and pilot.settings (theme and preferences). Signing out removes the token. The app also stores its own files in your browser so it opens quickly and can start offline; that never includes your tasks. It loads nothing from other websites.

Your rights, and how to use them

  • See and take your data: Settings, then "Your data", then "Download my data". You get one file with everything stored about you (never sign-in tokens, never anyone else's data).
  • Delete your data: Settings, then "Your data", then "Delete my account and data". Anything running is stopped, then your tasks, computers, devices, learned notes, usage counts and workspace are erased for good.
  • Correct it: you can rename your workspace and computers, and delete learned notes. To change your email, create a new account.
  • Object, restrict or withdraw: you can stop at any time by signing out or deleting your account; voice input is optional and off until you use it. If you want something else done with your data, ask us.
  • Complain: if you are in the EU, the UK or elsewhere with a data protection authority, you can complain to it. We would like the chance to help first.

If you cannot use the in-app tools, ask through the contact below and we will answer within 30 days.

How it is kept safe

  • Traffic to the server uses HTTPS. The link behind it between our servers is encrypted too.
  • No passwords exist to leak. Sign-in codes work once and expire (15 minutes for email links, 10 minutes for pairing), and wrong guesses are rate-limited.
  • Sign-in tokens are shown once and only a hash is stored. You can revoke any device or computer.
  • Every workspace is kept separate: asking for someone else's data answers "not found".
  • The safety rules (which apps, folders and sites are allowed, what needs your approval) are enforced by your computer and the server, not left to the AI model. Risky steps ask you first, and an emergency stop is always available in Settings.

No service is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that puts your data at risk, we will tell the people affected without undue delay.

Children, other countries, and changes

Children. Deskhand is not meant for children under 13 (under 16 where the law sets that age). If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Other countries. The server runs on a computer in the owner's home, and the services above (the AI model provider, email and speech services) may be in other countries, including the United States. By using Deskhand you understand your data can be processed there.

Changes. If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell signed-in people in the app when the change matters.

Contact

Questions, requests or concerns about privacy: open an issue at github.com/khanfawwaz/deskhand/issues. Please do not put private details in a public issue: say that you have a privacy request and we will arrange a private way to continue.
