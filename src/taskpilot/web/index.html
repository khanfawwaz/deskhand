Welcome to Deskhand

Tell your computer what to do, by voice or text, from anywhere. It does the clicking and typing for you.

  • Works on your own computer, not in the cloud
  • Asks you first before anything risky
  • No password: we email you a sign-in link

Check your inbox. Tap the link in the email, or type the code from it here.

Connect your first computer

Deskhand works on a Windows computer that has a small helper installed. It takes about a minute.

  1. Press the button to get your one-line install command.
  2. On the Windows computer, open PowerShell and paste it.
  3. Wait: the computer appears here by itself.
